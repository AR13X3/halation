Privacy policy: Halation on Pinterest

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Halation is a free, open-source tool for making backgrounds. The maintainer runs an integration that publishes images made with Halation to the maintainer's own Pinterest account, using the Pinterest API.

Whose account it uses. Only the maintainer's own Pinterest account. It's not offered to other people, and no one else can connect an account to it.

What it does on Pinterest. It reads that account's boards and Pins, publishes new Pins to its board automatically on a schedule, and reads performance figures for those Pins (impressions, saves and clicks).

What it keeps. The published images, their titles and descriptions, and the IDs of the Pins and board. It also keeps conclusions drawn from the performance figures, such as how often each style should come up. It doesn't keep the figures themselves.

What it doesn't collect. It doesn't collect or store information about people who view or save the Pins, and it doesn't use cookies or tracking of its own. Pinterest's own privacy policy covers your use of Pinterest.

Access tokens. The Pinterest access tokens are stored encrypted on a private server the maintainer controls. They're never published or shared.

Sharing. Nothing from this integration is sold or shared with third parties. Alerts about the integration go to a private channel that only the maintainer uses.

Links from Pins. A Pin's link opens the design in the Halation web app. Privacy in the Halation guide explains what the app counts.

Removal and questions. The maintainer can remove the Pins and revoke the integration's access at any time. To ask about anything here, open an issue on GitHub.
